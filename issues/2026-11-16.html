What's new in the swamp binary

From Swamp Weekly, Nov 20, with Seth

  • Restarts that keep your runs going

    A planned restart now waits for each workflow run to reach a step boundary, parks it, and resumes it automatically afterwards. No more manual recovery after restarting mid-deploy, which makes rolling deploys practical.

  • Nested workflows inherit placement

    A child workflow now runs on the same worker as its parent instead of falling back to the orchestrator. A child can still opt out and run on the orchestrator.

  • Safer config management over SSH

    Firewall rules are checked before they are applied, so a change is much less likely to cut your own connection. systemd units can be removed as well as created, failed service starts are detected properly, and the SSH extension can require pinned host keys.

  • Approve and resume in one step

    One call replaces the separate approve and resume calls: fewer round trips, and fewer runs left waiting because resume was never called.

  • Many hosted workspaces behind one serve · in progress

    One service that knows about all your workspaces and routes swamp serve instances through it.
